GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

Can AI Checker prove whether a video is AI-generated? +

No. Even advanced detection models can produce false positives and false negatives. Results are best treated as probabilistic indicators, not conclusive proof. This version distinguishes model results from metadata evidence.

Does it work with TikTok, Instagram, and Facebook? +

Yes, by uploading videos saved from those platforms. Public URLs are supported on a best-effort basis when the platform allows retrieval, but some posts and accounts are inaccessible. Private posts and login bypassing are not supported.

Are my videos kept on your servers? +

Uploaded videos are processed in temporary storage and deleted after analysis or failure. Completed report metadata is held in memory for about 20 minutes. When the optional AI provider is enabled, a shortened video sample is sent to the analysis provider.

What if it says “inconclusive”? +

It means there is not enough reliable evidence to classify the clip. If visual AI detection is not configured, AI Checker will analyze file facts but will not invent a percentage or prediction.
