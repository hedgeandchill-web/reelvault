Is ReelVault free to use?

Yes, ReelVault is free to use. Paste a public Instagram video link to preview and download when the video is accessible.

Do I need to log in to Instagram?

No. ReelVault never asks for your Instagram username or password. It only supports directly accessible public Reels and video posts.

Can I download private Instagram videos?

No. Private, deleted, restricted, or login-required content isn't supported. Instagram can also block access to some public content.

Will my video be high quality?

We attempt the best available MP4 video stream. Actual quality depends on what Instagram makes publicly accessible, and some videos may be unavailable.

Where does my downloaded video go?

Usually your device's Downloads folder. On iPhone, check Safari Downloads or the Files app. You can then save the clip to Photos if you want.

Can I repost the videos I save?

Only download and use content you own or have permission to save. Reposting or commercial reuse may require permission from the copyright holder.
